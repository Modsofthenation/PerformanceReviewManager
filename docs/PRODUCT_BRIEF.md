# Product Brief — Optimal EM Performance Review Tool

## One-liner

A living engineering dossier that becomes a structured, calibratable review packet — with AI that drafts from evidence, not vibes.

## Who it is for

Engineering managers running reviews for IC and EM reports inside mid-size to large product orgs.

## Core loop

```
year-round evidence → cycle templates → self/peer/manager forms
  → AI-assisted writing desk → calibration room → share → history trends
```

## Pillars

1. **Templates as versioned contracts** — competencies, anchors, scales, self/peer/manager/upward forms; pinned per cycle.
2. **Dossier** — goals, feedback, achievements, optional GitHub/Linear signals; reviews attach evidence to scores.
3. **Writing desk** — packet + draft split view optimized for ≤45 min/person.
4. **Calibration room** — distributions, side-by-side, decision audit log (no forced stack-rank by default).
5. **History** — person timelines, competency sparklines, stagnation alerts.
6. **AI** — digest, grounded drafts, peer themes, bias lint, calib talking points; never auto-submits; always cites.

## Explicit non-goals

Comp planning, stack-rank machinery, Slack DM surveillance, replacing HRIS.

## Build order

Foundations → templates/dossier/cycle → calibration/history → AI → integrations → enterprise hardening.
