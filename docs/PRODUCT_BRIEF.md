# Product Brief — Optimal EM Performance Review Tool

## One-liner

A living engineering dossier that becomes a structured, calibratable review packet — with AI that drafts from evidence, not vibes.

## Who it is for

Engineering managers running reviews for IC and EM reports inside mid-size to large product orgs.

## Core loop

```
year-round evidence → cycle templates → self/peer/manager forms
  → AI-assisted writing desk → calibration room → share → history trends
```

## Pillars

1. **Templates as versioned contracts** — competencies, anchors, scales, self/peer/manager/upward/**promotion** forms; pinned per cycle.
2. **Role frameworks** — upload engineering R&R docs, structure RoleDefinitions, tag each person; **current + next role text is the evaluation basis**.
3. **Dossier** — goals, feedback, achievements, **uploaded documents**, optional GitHub/Linear signals and **Meet/Drive meeting summaries**; reviews attach evidence to scores.
4. **Writing desk** — packet + draft with role-fit and next-level gap checklist; ≤45 min/person.
5. **Calibration room** — distributions, side-by-side, decision audit log (no forced stack-rank by default).
6. **History** — person timelines, competency sparklines, stagnation alerts.
7. **AI** — digest, grounded drafts, **role gap analysis**, promo drafts, peer themes, bias lint; never auto-submits; always cites.

## Explicit non-goals

Comp planning, stack-rank machinery, Slack DM surveillance, replacing HRIS.

## Build order

Local desktop foundations (Tauri + SQLite) → templates/dossier/cycle → calibration/history → AI → integrations → hardening.
